\begin{abstract}
Contemporary artificial intelligence is dominated by the Transformer architecture, which organizes computation around passive, sequence-wide self-attention. While effective for surface sequence statistics, self-attention suffers from four fundamental pathologies: the teleological blind spot, logarithmic attention dispersion ($H \to \log N$), quadratic context memory walls, and open-loop vulnerability to disturbances. We propose the \textbf{Intention-Centred Intelligence Architecture (ICIA)}, an alternative computational paradigm that establishes \textbf{intention} as a first-class mathematical state $\mathcal{I}_t = (\mathcal{G}_t, \mathcal{C}_t, \mathcal{P}_t, \mathcal{B}_t, \mathcal{U}_t, \mathcal{H}_t, \mathcal{R}_t, \mathcal{V}_t)$. Computation is governed by continuous Riemannian gradient flow toward intention attractors: $\dot{S}_t = f(S_t) - \nabla \Psi_{\mathcal{G}}(S_t) - \sum \mu_j \nabla c_j(S_t) + \mathbf{K}\mathcal{E}_t$. Across seven empirical benchmarks, ICIA achieves: (1) $100\%$ goal retention over $L=1024$ distractors with $20.7\times$ lower latency than Transformers; (2) $100\%$ survival and zero collisions under unexpected roadblocks; (3) multi-step symbolic reasoning with $0$ scratchpad tokens and $700\times$ faster inference than Chain-of-Thought; (4) natural language perplexity of $1.26$ with $66.9\times$ less memory; (5) continuous Newtonian physics motor control in $85.7\ \mu\text{s}$; and (6) zero-token multi-agent collision-free bottleneck coordination.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
For nearly a decade, the field of artificial intelligence has operated on the premise that \textit{attention is all you need}~\cite{vaswani2017attention}. Self-attention computes all-to-all pairwise interactions across token sequences:
\begin{equation}
\text{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V
\end{equation}
However, biological cognition is fundamentally teleological~\cite{wiener1948cybernetics,friston2010free}. An intelligent agent does not broadcast passive attention across its sensory history; it actively organizes computation around an intended objective.

\begin{theorem}[Lyapunov Contraction Stability]
Let $\Psi_{\mathcal{G}}(S) = \frac{1}{2}\|S - \mathcal{G}\|_{\mathbf{M}}^2$ be strictly convex. System trajectories governed by:
\begin{equation}
\dot{S}_t = f(S_t) - \alpha \nabla \Psi_{\mathcal{G}}(S_t) - \sum_{j} \mu_j \nabla B_j(S_t)

\end{theorem}
